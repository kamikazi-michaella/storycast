About StoryCast

StoryCast is a small home for audio and video storytelling. We believe a story belongs to everyone, so access is built in from the start, not added at the end.

Every story has captions or a transcript, so you can watch it, listen to it, or read it, whichever works best for you.

Accessibility features

  • Captions and transcripts

    Every video has captions, and every video and audio story has a full written transcript of the dialogue, narration and key visual action.

  • Keyboard navigation

    Every link, button and media control works without a mouse. A skip link takes you straight to the main content, and a clear outline shows where you are.

  • Readable design

    Text and controls meet WCAG 2.1 AA colour contrast, and the layout adapts to phones, tablets and desktops.

  • Screen reader support

    Pages use landmarks, a logical heading order and labelled controls, with ARIA only where plain HTML is not enough.

  • Respect for motion settings

    If your device is set to reduce motion, StoryCast avoids animation.

How to engage with our stories

  1. Choose a story from the featured stories on the home page.
  2. Open the story page and watch with captions, listen, or read the transcript.
  3. Tell us what worked and what did not using the contact details below.
Keyboard controls for the media players
Key What it does
Tab Move to the next link or control
Enter or Space Press a button, or play and pause the focused player
Left and right arrows Skip backward or forward in the focused player
Up and down arrows Change the volume of the focused player
