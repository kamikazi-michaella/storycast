About & access

A small home for stories that include everyone.

StoryCast is an experiment in accessible storytelling. Each story is built to be watched, heard or read, without a separate, lesser version for disabled audiences.

We believe access belongs at the start of the design process, not at the end. That means captions, transcripts, keyboard support and readable type are part of the story from day one.

Accessibility features

What is built in.

The details that make the experience work for more people, without getting in the way.

  • 01

    Captions and transcripts

    Every video has captions. Every video and audio story has a full written transcript of the dialogue, narration and key visual action.

  • 02

    Live transcript sync

    The transcript highlights the current line and scrolls with the media, so you can follow along without losing your place.

  • 03

    Keyboard navigation

    Every link, button and media control works without a mouse. A skip link takes you straight to the main content, and a clear outline shows where you are.

  • 04

    Readable design

    Text and controls meet WCAG 2.1 AA colour contrast, and the layout adapts to phones, tablets and desktops.

  • 05

    Screen reader support

    Pages use landmarks, a logical heading order and labelled controls, with ARIA only where plain HTML is not enough.

  • 06

    Respect for motion settings

    If your device is set to reduce motion, StoryCast avoids smooth scrolling, animation and other movement.

How to engage

Find your way in.

Choose the format that fits the moment. The story is the same; the route is yours.

  1. 01

    Choose a story from the featured stories on the home page.

  2. 02

    Open the story page and watch with captions, listen, or read the live transcript.

  3. 03

    Tell us what worked and what did not using the contact details below.

Keyboard controls for the media players
Key What it does
Tab Move to the next link or control
Enter or Space Press a button, or play and pause the focused player
Left and right arrows Skip backward or forward in the focused player
Up and down arrows Change the volume of the focused player
